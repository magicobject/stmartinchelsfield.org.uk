    Site updates

    Internal build changelog — not linked from anywhere on the site.

    Build 2026.09.11.004 Greg Wright 11 September 2026

    Added a step to Setting Up Your Laptop: turn on two-factor authentication before requesting collaborator access. Noted the same requirement in CLAUDE.md's multi-developer workflow section, and that this repo (a personal-account repo, not an organisation) has no GitHub setting to enforce or verify it automatically — it's a policy to apply by hand.

    Build 2026.09.11.003 Greg Wright 11 September 2026

    Added a concrete step to Setting Up Your Laptop: once someone has created a GitHub account, they need to email their account name to web@mediawright.uk to be added as a collaborator, rather than the previous vague "ask whoever manages this repository".

    Build 2026.09.11.002 Greg Wright 11 September 2026

    Redrew the favicon and social-preview image's church mark as a recognisable splay-foot spire — flared base, straight taper to a point, ball-and-weathervane finial — matching the real spire visible in the homepage's own hero photo, in place of the previous generic arch.

    Build 2026.09.11.001 Greg Wright 11 September 2026

    Scraped the parish's own combined Google Calendar (linked from its interim WordPress site) for 1 September–31 December 2026 and used it to populate the calendar with real services and events, replacing the guessed weekly pattern from build 001. Rewrote Services to describe the real rota (1st/3rd/2nd/4th Sunday pattern, Well Hill Mission's fortnightly services, the Taizé and choir/ringing practice schedule) instead of a flat weekly list. Confirmed real meeting times for Toddler Group, Choir and Bellringers on Groups & Activities. Added the Parish Safeguarding Officer (Steve McCann) and the Diocesan Safeguarding Advisor (Caroline Smith) to Safeguarding and Clergy & Contacts, and added churchwardens/bookings email addresses to Contact — all sourced from the parish's own site rather than a third-party directory.

    Build 2026.09.10.004 Greg Wright 10 September 2026

    Added a Facebook icon to the footer's "Get in touch" column, linking to facebook.com/ChelsfieldStMartins, supplied directly.

    Build 2026.09.10.003 Greg Wright 10 September 2026

    Replaced the homepage's line-illustration with a real photo of the church, supplied directly (public/img/hero-church.jpg) — see the homepage. Fixed button text sitting visibly above center in every pill button (.btn/.btn-donate/.btn-primary/.btn-outline) — the inherited body line-height was padding unevenly around text with no descenders; set line-height: 1 plus a small asymmetric top/bottom padding split instead.

    Build 2026.09.10.002 Greg Wright 10 September 2026

    New palette (navy/gold/rose, replacing the first build's green/terracotta — a closer look showed too much overlap with other MediaWright sites), see public/css/style.css's opening comment. Added a Google Maps embed to the homepage and Visit Us (address-based, so no coordinate-guessing — see CLAUDE.md's Security section), with frame-src added to the CSP. Donate now links directly to the parish's real Parish Giving Scheme page, supplied by the site owner rather than sourced. Added a header-level X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow alongside the existing meta tag and robots.txt, since the repo is now also live-previewable at a Cloudflare Workers subdomain.

    Build 2026.09.10.001 Greg Wright 10 September 2026

    First build of the site: the full templated pipeline (build, search index, security headers, accessibility tests), all core pages, an interactive calendar, and a newsletter signup form. Content sourced from public directory listings for St Martin of Tours, Chelsfield — several facts (clergy beyond the Rector, exact opening hours, a Parish Giving Scheme reference) still need confirming with the parish before this goes live; see Technical Details.
