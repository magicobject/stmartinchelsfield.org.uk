    When something goes wrong

    Nothing here is ever permanently broken.

    A change looks wrong

    Just tell Claude what looks off — "the phone number in the footer is wrong now" — and ask it to fix or revert that specific change.

    Revert to how things were

    Every change is its own save point in the site's history. Ask Claude to "undo the last change" or "go back to how the Contact page looked before," and it can restore exactly that.

    Still stuck?

    Contact whoever manages this repository (see Technical Details) — nothing is unrecoverable, so there's no need to panic.
