    Safeguarding, care and nurture

    Everyone should be safe at St Martin's — children, young people and adults alike.

    Who to contact

    Call 999 if a child or adult is in immediate danger of significant or serious harm.

    If you're worried about a current or historic incidence of abuse — sexual, financial, emotional, controlling behaviour, bullying, or domestic abuse — you don't need to suffer alone.

    Our parish

    A named Parish Safeguarding Officer has not yet been published for this site — see the note below. In the meantime, contact the Rector, Revd John Tranter, who can direct any concern to the right person straight away.
