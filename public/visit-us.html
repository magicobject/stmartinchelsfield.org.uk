    Visit us

    St Martin of Tours sits just outside Chelsfield village, on the edge of the London Borough of Bromley.

    Getting here

    • By car Just off the M25 — use the postcode BR6 7SN in your sat nav.
    • By train Nearest station is Chelsfield, on the Sevenoaks line — a short walk from there, or take the R3 bus from Orpington station.
    • On foot Walkable from both Chelsfield village and Orpington.

    Opening hours

    The church is usually open during the day for private prayer and quiet reflection — please check with the parish office if you're travelling especially to visit outside a service time, as hours can vary.

    Facilities & access

    Toilets and gardens are on site. Step-free access, parking availability and hearing loop provision haven't been confirmed for this site yet — please contact us in advance if any of these affect your visit, and we'll let you know what's available.

    Concerned about a child or adult's safety? Call 999 in an emergency.
