    Groups & activities

    St Martin's is more than Sunday mornings — several groups meet regularly, and the Brass Crosby Rooms are available to hire.

    Our groups

    Tuesdays, 10:30am–12:30pm

    Toddler Group

    A weekly session for parents, carers and pre-school children — play, songs and a cup of tea. Meets in the Brass Crosby Rooms.

    Fridays, 8pm

    Choir

    Leads the music at our services — new voices are always welcome. Currently running to 13 November 2026; check the calendar for what's next.

    Mondays, 7:45pm

    Bellringers

    Ring the tower bells for services and practise regularly — beginners welcome.

    Ladies Group

    Fellowship, talks and outings throughout the year. Get in touch for the current meeting schedule.

    Toddler Group, Choir and Bellringers' times come from the parish's own published calendar. The Ladies Group's meeting time wasn't on it — get in touch and we'll add it here once confirmed.

    Hire the Brass Crosby Rooms

    Our 2007 extension gives Chelsfield a modern, flexible meeting space alongside the historic church — see its own story. Get in touch to check availability and rates.

    Volunteer

    From welcoming visitors to helping with the Brass Crosby Rooms, running a group, or joining the PCC, there's always a way to help. Get in touch and we'll point you in the right direction.
