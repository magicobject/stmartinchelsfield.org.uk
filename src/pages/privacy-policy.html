Privacy policy

How we handle personal details: newsletter sign-ups, and the people named on our Clergy & contacts page.

What we collect

When you sign up for our newsletter, we collect your email address and, optionally, your first name.

Why we collect it

Solely to send you our newsletter. Signing up does not send a confirmation email — you're subscribed immediately, and your details are stored with our email provider for that purpose only.

How to unsubscribe

Every newsletter email includes an unsubscribe link. You can also email the parish at any time and we'll remove you.

People named on this website

Our Clergy & contacts page shows the names, roles, photos, email addresses and, for three people, telephone numbers of parish office-holders, so that parishioners and visitors can reach them. These details are already published by the parish on A Church Near You. Email addresses are not shown as text on the page, to make them harder for automated programs to collect. If you are listed and want your details corrected or removed, email the parish and we will act on it promptly.

Who else sees it

Your details are only used to send the newsletter and are not shared with any other organisation, except our email delivery provider, acting solely on our instructions.

Questions

Contact the parish with any question about how we handle your data.
