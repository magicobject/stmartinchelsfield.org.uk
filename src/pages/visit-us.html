Getting here

  • By car Just off the M25 — use the postcode BR6 7SN in your sat nav. what3words address: finger.amuse.rate
  • By train Nearest station is Chelsfield, on the Sevenoaks line — a 1 mile walk from there, or take the R7 bus from Orpington station to Chelsfield Village.
  • On foot Walkable from Chelsfield Village and Station.

Opening hours

The church is normally open each day for private prayer and reflection from 9.30am until 5pm (or dusk when that is earlier).

Facilities & access

Toilets and gardens are on site. Step-free access, large car park and hearing loop during services.
