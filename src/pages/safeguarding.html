Our commitment

The Parish of Chelsfield is committed to implementing the House of Bishops' safeguarding policies and good practice guidance. Our Parochial Church Council agreed our safeguarding policy on 23 July 2026. It promises a safe and caring place for all; a named Parish Safeguarding Officer; safe recruitment, training and support for everyone with responsibility for children, young people and adults; prompt action on every concern; pastoral care for victims and survivors; and care for, and monitoring of, anyone who may pose a risk to others. Everyone who works within this church community agrees to abide by it.
