Services

A settled weekly pattern of worship, with room for everyone from quiet reflection to Sunday best.

Sundays

The 9:30am service rotates through the month — check the calendar for what's on a specific date, or use this pattern as a guide:

  • 1st & 3rd Sundays 9:30am Holy Communion
  • 2nd Sunday 9:30am Family Worship
  • 4th Sunday 9:30am Morning Worship
  • A 5th Sunday, when the month has one, has no fixed pattern — see the calendar

In the evening, 6:30pm is usually Choral Evensong, except the 2nd Sunday, which is Sung Eucharist (BCP).

Well Hill Mission Church

Our sister congregation at Well Hill meets on the 2nd and 4th Sundays, 11am:

  • 2nd Sunday Morning Prayer
  • 4th Sunday Holy Communion

Wednesdays

  • 9:30am Holy Communion (BCP), every week
  • 7pm, 1st Wednesday Taizé — a quiet service of prayers and readings

Music and bells

Fridays, 8pm. Currently running to 13 November 2026 — nothing further is booked in yet beyond that date, so check the calendar nearer the time.

Bell ringing practice

Mondays, 7:45pm, ongoing.

These times come directly from the parish's own published calendar (checked 11 September 2026) rather than a third-party directory, but service patterns do change — the calendar always shows the specific service booked for a given date, and is the more reliable source if the two ever disagree.
