Technical details

Optional reading. You don't need any of this to make a change; Claude already knows it all.

Hosting

The site is deployed to Cloudflare Workers, the same platform used for other MediaWright-built parish sites. For now it is a preview at smc.mediawright.uk, not yet on the parish's own domain. See "About this build" in the repository's CLAUDE.md for why.

The templated build

Every page in public/ is generated from templates/ and src/pages/ by scripts/build.mjs — never hand-edited directly. This keeps the header, footer, contact details and safeguarding information consistent across every page from one source.

Testing

Every page is scanned for accessibility issues with axe-core, and a Playwright test suite checks navigation, forms, the calendar, and search. npm audit checks for known vulnerabilities in dependencies. All of this runs before every push.

Newsletter signup

The signup form posts to a Cloudflare Worker endpoint that talks to MailerLite — but no MailerLite account has been connected yet (see wrangler.jsonc's placeholder group id). The form and its tests work end-to-end; a real signup won't until that's configured.

Where each fact lives

Each kind of fact is kept in one place, so one change updates every page that uses it. You never need to open these files or know their names: Claude does. They are listed here for the curious.

  • People The clergy and officers on Clergy & contacts, with their photos, are in people.config.mjs.
  • Services & events The calendar comes from the parish’s Google Calendar: change it there, then ask for the calendar to be re-imported (npm run import:calendar, which writes events.imported.mjs; events.config.mjs combines it with any hand-added events). Only dates, times, titles and places are copied, never the notes, and only event titles on an approved list are published; anything unfamiliar is held back until a person approves it, so private events such as funerals and weddings stay off the site.
  • Address & phone The church address, the Rectory phone number and the parish email are in site.config.mjs.
  • Gallery photos Added in gallery.config.mjs (each photo needs a description for people who cannot see it).
  • Newsletter Issues are listed in newsletter.config.mjs.
  • Safeguarding The contacts are on the Safeguarding page (safeguarding.html) and the policy wording is in safeguarding-policy.html, word for word from the PCC-agreed document.
  • History & churchyard The text is in about.html and churchyard.html.
  • What's still to do The plan for the parish's requirements, with what is done and what we are waiting for, is docs/plans/2026-10-parish-requirements.md.
