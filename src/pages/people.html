Keeping safe, and keeping this page right

Beware of impersonation. We will never ask you for money, gift cards or passwords by email, text or phone. If a message claims to be from someone on this page and asks for any of those, don't reply — let the Churchwarden know.

Safeguarding concerns should go through the official routes on our Safeguarding page (including the Diocese and the NSPCC), not to an individual's personal inbox. In an emergency, call 999.

Your details. The names, photos, email addresses and phone numbers above are used with the knowledge of the people shown. If you are listed here and would like your details corrected or removed, email the parish and we will act on it promptly. See our privacy policy.
