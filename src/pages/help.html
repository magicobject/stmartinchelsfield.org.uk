How to update this website

A plain-English guide for the website team. You say what you'd like changed; Claude makes it happen.

The short version

  • Your job Say clearly what you'd like changed, in everyday words. No coding knowledge is needed.
  • Claude's job Make the change, check it, and put it on the website.
  • Stuck? Once Claude is up and running, you can ask it for help at any time: "I'm not sure what to do next", "What does this mean?" or "Something looks wrong."
