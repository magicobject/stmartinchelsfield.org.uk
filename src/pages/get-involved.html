Groups & activities

St Martin's is more than Sunday mornings — several groups meet regularly, and the Brass Crosby Room is available to hire.

Our groups

Tuesdays, 10.30am – 12.00 noon

Toddler Group

A weekly session during school term time for parents, carers and pre-school children — play, songs and a cup of tea. Meets in the Brass Crosby Room.

Fridays, 8pm

Choir

Leads the music at our services — new voices are always welcome.

Mondays, 7:45pm

Bellringers

Ring the tower bells for services and practise regularly — beginners welcome.

3rd Saturday of most months

Messy Church

Meets monthly on the 3rd Saturday of most months at St Mary's Church, Green Street Green (part of the same Benefice). Please check the calendar.

1st & 3rd Wednesdays, 2.30pm

Community Café

Meets at 2.30pm on the first and third Wednesday of the month at Chelsfield Village Hall. Although it is not a Church group many church members are involved with it and all are welcome.

First Saturday, 9–11am

Churchyard Working Party

Meets on the first Saturday morning each month, from 9–11am, to keep our beautiful churchyard looking good. All are welcome.

Ladies Group

Fellowship, talks and outings throughout the year. Get in touch for the current meeting schedule.
