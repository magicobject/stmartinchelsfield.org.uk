Groups & activities

St Martin's is more than Sunday mornings — several groups meet regularly, and the Brass Crosby Rooms are available to hire.

Our groups

Toddler Group

A weekly session for parents, carers and pre-school children — play, songs and a cup of tea.

Choir

Leads the music at our services — new voices are always welcome.

Bellringers

Ring the tower bells for services and practise regularly — beginners welcome.

Ladies Group

Fellowship, talks and outings throughout the year.

Meeting times for these groups weren't published anywhere this site could source them from — get in touch for the current schedule, and we'll add it here.
