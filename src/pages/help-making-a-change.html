Making a change

Your job is to say clearly what you want. Claude's job is to make it happen.

Just describe what you want changed

Open Claude Desktop in this project, click on the Claude Code icon, then in a new session describe the change in plain English — "update the Wednesday service time to 10am" or "add a new photo to the About page." Claude edits the right source file, rebuilds the site, and shows you the result.

How to ask clearly

The clearer you are, the less Claude has to guess. It helps to say:

  • Where Which page, and which part of it: "On the Groups page, in the Messy Church box".
  • What exactly The old and the new: "Change the time from 3pm to 4pm."
  • The exact words If the wording matters, type it out just as you want it to appear.
  • One thing at a time Small, separate requests are easier to check, and easier to undo.
  • Photos Say where the photo is on your computer, what it shows, and that you have permission to use it. Claude will resize it and write a description for people who can't see it.

Ask Claude for help at any time

You never have to work it out alone. If you're not sure what to ask for, or what something means, just ask Claude: "What can I change on the Visit us page?", "Explain what you just did in plain English", or "Show me what it looks like before it goes live."

What happens next

Claude makes your change on a private copy first, then runs a set of automatic checks: every page still works, is easy to read and use, and is safe. Only when all of those pass does it send the change on. Simple changes then go live by themselves, usually within a couple of minutes. Bigger changes wait for someone to approve them first (see When it goes wrong). You don't need to do anything technical at any point.

Common questions

  • Can I break it? Not permanently. Every change is saved separately, so any of them can be undone. See When it goes wrong.
  • Do I need to know code? No. Describe what you want in everyday words.
  • Which file holds what? You never need to know. Claude does. (The curious can see the list under Technical details.)
  • How long until it's live? Usually within a minute or two of the change being approved.
