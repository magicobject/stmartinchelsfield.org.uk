Making a change

What happens, step by step, when you ask Claude to update the site.

Just describe what you want changed

Open Claude Desktop in this project and describe the change in plain English — "update the Wednesday service time to 10am" or "add a new photo to the About page." Claude edits the right source file, rebuilds the site, and shows you the result.

What Claude checks before anything goes live

Every change runs through the same checks: the automated test suite (including an accessibility scan of every page), a dependency security check, and a rebuild that regenerates every page from its source. Nothing is pushed live until those pass.

Where each fact lives

Each kind of fact is kept in one place, so one change updates every page that uses it. You don't need to open these files, but naming the right one helps Claude make the right change.

  • People The clergy and officers on Clergy & contacts, with their photos, are in people.config.mjs.
  • Services & events The calendar is in events.config.mjs.
  • Address & phone The church address, the Rectory phone number and the parish email are in site.config.mjs.
  • Gallery photos Added in gallery.config.mjs (each photo needs a description for people who cannot see it).
  • Newsletter Issues are listed in newsletter.config.mjs.
  • Safeguarding The contacts are on the Safeguarding page (safeguarding.html) and the policy wording is in safeguarding-policy.html, word for word from the PCC-agreed document.
  • History & churchyard The text is in about.html and churchyard.html.
  • What's still to do The plan for the parish's requirements, with what is done and what we are waiting for, is docs/plans/2026-10-parish-requirements.md.

Common questions

  • Can I break it? Not permanently — every change is a new, separate save point. See When It Goes Wrong.
  • Do I need to know code? No — describe what you want in plain English.
  • How long until it's live? Once pushed, Cloudflare deploys automatically, usually within a minute or two.
